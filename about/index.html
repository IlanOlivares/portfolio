I'm Ilan. I build websites in El Paso.

Independent web designer and developer. For more than ten years I've helped businesses, churches, nonprofits, public agencies and publications show up online the way they deserve to.

Why I do this

Most organizations are better than their websites. They do careful, meaningful work, then send people to a site that's slow, confusing or years out of date. I like closing that gap: finding what makes a business good and making it obvious to anyone who finds it online.

I care about the look and about the details that make a site work: the words, the load time, the button on a phone screen. Beautiful and unusable helps no one. Neither does functional and forgettable.

How I work

I keep things small on purpose. When you hire me, you get me: on the first call, in the design, in the code and on the other end of the email when something needs to change. No hand-offs and no one learning your business secondhand.

My work spans magazine sites that sell advertising, church websites and livestreams, real estate launches, landing pages built from other designers' files, and public information pages and alert systems for residents. The approach stays the same: simplify the message, elevate the presentation, make the next step obvious and build something that can grow.

Rooted in El Paso

I live and work in El Paso, and I want local businesses to compete with anyone. I work with clients in English and Spanish. I also work with clients in Las Cruces, across Texas and New Mexico, and around the country.
