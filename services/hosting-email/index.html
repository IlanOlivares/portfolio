Hosting, domains & email

Managed hosting, DNS, SSL and professional business email, set up properly and looked after.

Why it matters

When email stops working or a domain expires, business stops. Yet most organizations have their infrastructure spread across accounts nobody remembers setting up. We bring it together, document it and keep it healthy.

What we manage

We host your site on fast, secure infrastructure with SSL and backups, manage your domain and DNS, and set up business email on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 with the authentication records that keep your messages out of spam. If you're moving from another provider, we plan and run the migration so nothing gets lost.

Everything stays in your name and is documented, so you always have control and a clear picture of what you own.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Managed website hosting
  • SSL certificates
  • Domain registration and renewals
  • DNS management
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 setup
  • Email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Migrations from current providers
  • Backups, uptime monitoring and support

Questions

Can you move our email without losing messages?

Yes. We plan the migration, move mailboxes and switch DNS at a quiet time, so your team keeps working with minimal disruption. Read our Google Workspace migration guide.

Will the domain and accounts be in our name?

Yes. We set up infrastructure in your organization's name and document access, so you always have control.
