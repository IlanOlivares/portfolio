Your site should keep up with you

Hours change, people change, events come and go. A site that isn't updated starts to look abandoned, and wrong information costs you calls. With website care, you send the change and it gets made right, without you logging in to figure it out.

  • Domain, hosting and SSL check-ins

Common questions

Straight answers before you get started.

Do you maintain sites you didn't build?

Yes. I review the site first and share what I find before we agree on anything.

How fast are updates made?

Small updates are usually quick, and urgent items like closures or emergency notices come first.

Is there a long-term contract?

Care can be ongoing or as needed. We'll choose what fits how often your site changes.

Need a reliable person for your website?

Tell me what you do and what you need. I'll reply personally with next steps and an honest read on whether I'm the right fit. The first consultation is free.
