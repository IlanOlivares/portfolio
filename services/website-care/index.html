Your website should keep up with you

Hours change, staff changes, events come and go, and new services launch. A website that isn't updated starts to look abandoned, and outdated information costs you calls and trust. Website care means you send a message and the change gets made, correctly, without you having to log in and figure it out.

What I handle

  • Text, photo and page updates
  • New pages, landing pages and sections
  • Announcement and emergency alert bars
  • Ad placements and promotional spots
  • Broken links, layout issues and form fixes
  • Domain, hosting and SSL check-ins
  • Search Console monitoring and SEO upkeep

For sites I didn't build, too

Common questions

Straight answers about website care & support in El Paso.

Do you maintain websites you didn't build?

Yes. I'll review the site first and let you know what I find before we agree on anything.

How quickly are updates made?

Small updates are usually handled quickly, and urgent items like closures or emergency notices get priority.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

Care can be arranged ongoing or as needed. We'll pick what fits how often your site changes.

Need a reliable person for your website?

Tell me about your business and what you need. I'll reply personally with next steps and an honest take on whether I'm the right fit.
