How people find local businesses now

Search engines, map apps, voice assistants and AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity reward the same things: a fast, well-built website, clear statements about what you do and where, and consistent information everywhere you're listed.

Technical SEO

I fix what holds sites back: slow pages, missing titles and descriptions, broken links, weak mobile layouts, missing sitemaps and content search engines can't read. Then I connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools so you can see how people find you.

Built for AI answers

AI assistants recommend businesses they can understand and verify. I add schema markup describing your business, services and service area. I write FAQ content that answers real customer questions, publish an llms.txt summary for AI tools, allow AI crawlers and keep your name, location and services consistent across the web.

Beyond your website

Your Google Business Profile, Bing Places listing, reviews and directory listings carry real weight in maps and AI answers. I'll help you set them up right and keep them in sync with your site.

Straight talk

No one can guarantee a #1 ranking or control what an AI says, and anyone who promises it isn't being straight with you. What I can do is clear every obstacle, give search engines and AI tools trustworthy information, and help you build a reputation that earns visibility over time.

Common questions

Straight answers before you get started.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can force an AI to recommend a business. I make yours much easier for AI tools to understand and cite, with clear content, schema markup, consistent listings, crawler access and an llms.txt file.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show results in weeks. Competitive local rankings usually build over several months as your site, reviews and listings earn trust.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

For most local businesses, yes. It drives map results and is one of the strongest local signals. Service-area businesses can hide their address and still appear.

Is SEO included with a new website?

The technical foundations come with every site I build. Ongoing local SEO and content work can be added separately.
