How people find local businesses now

Someone looking for a service in El Paso might search Google, open Maps, ask a voice assistant or type the question into ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. All of them reward the same things: a fast, well-structured website, clear statements about what you do and where you do it, and consistent information across the web.

Technical SEO, done properly

I fix the foundations that hold sites back: slow pages, missing titles and descriptions, broken links, poor mobile layouts, missing sitemaps and pages search engines can't read. Then I set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools so you can see how people find you.

Built for AI answers

AI assistants recommend businesses they can understand and verify. I add structured data (schema markup) describing your business, services and service area. I write FAQ content that directly answers the questions customers ask, add an llms.txt file that summarizes your business for AI tools, and make sure your name, location and services match everywhere they appear online.

Local presence beyond your website

Your Google Business Profile, Bing Places listing, reviews and directory listings carry a lot of weight in both map results and AI answers. I'll help you set them up correctly and keep them consistent with your site.

An honest note

No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google or control what an AI assistant says. Anyone who promises that isn't being straight with you. What I can do is remove every obstacle, give search engines and AI tools clear, trustworthy information, and help you build a reputation that earns visibility over time.

Common questions

Straight answers about local seo & ai search visibility in El Paso.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can force an AI to recommend a business. I can make your business far easier for AI tools to understand and cite, with clear content, structured data, consistent listings, AI crawler access and an llms.txt file. These are the signals AI assistants rely on.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Rankings for competitive local searches usually build over several months as your site, reviews and listings gain trust.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

For most local businesses, yes. It drives map results and is one of the strongest signals for local searches. Service-area businesses can hide their address and still appear.

Is SEO included when you build my website?

The technical foundations are included in every site I build. Ongoing content and local visibility work can be added as a separate service.
