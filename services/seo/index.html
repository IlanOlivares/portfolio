SEO & AI search

Technical SEO, structured data and content that get you found on Google, Maps and in AI answers.

Why it matters

Search engines and AI assistants reward the same fundamentals: a fast, well-structured site, content that answers real questions, and consistent, verifiable information about who you are and where you work. Get those right and visibility compounds over time.

How we approach it

We start with a technical audit and fix what holds you back. Then we add structured data that describes your organization, services and locations, shape content around the questions your customers ask, and set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools so progress is measurable.

For AI search, also called generative engine optimization, we make your site easy for assistants to read and cite: clear entity information, FAQ content, an llms.txt file and access for AI crawlers. We also help you keep Google Business Profile, Bing Places and key directories consistent.

An honest note

No one can guarantee rankings or control what an AI says. Anyone who promises otherwise isn't being straight with you. What we can do is remove every obstacle and give search engines and AI the clearest, most trustworthy picture of your business.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes
  • Schema markup and structured data
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile
  • Content strategy and FAQ content
  • AI search readiness and llms.txt
  • Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
  • Listing and citation consistency
  • Monthly reporting

Questions

Can you get us recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can force an AI to recommend a business. We make yours much easier to understand and cite, using the signals AI assistants rely on.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Competitive rankings typically build over several months.
