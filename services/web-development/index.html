Web development

Fast, accessible, hand-built sites on modern stacks, from static to Webflow to headless CMS.

Why it matters

Speed, accessibility and reliability aren't technical niceties. They decide whether visitors stay, whether search engines rank you and whether your site works for everyone. Heavy page builders and plugin stacks trade all three for convenience.

How we approach it

We choose the stack around your needs and your team, not habit. Most of our sites are hand-built for performance, with content editing where you need it. We integrate the tools you rely on, such as forms, CRMs, booking, payments and analytics, and test across browsers, devices and assistive technology before launch.

Our own website scores 100 across Google Lighthouse on mobile. We hold client builds to the same standard.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Webflow development and embeds
  • Headless CMS integration
  • Forms, CRM and booking integrations
  • Performance optimization
  • Accessibility (WCAG) implementation
  • Analytics and event tracking
  • Cross-browser and device testing

Questions

Will we be able to edit the site ourselves?

If you need to, yes. We'll recommend a platform with editing built in, such as Webflow or a headless CMS, and train your team.

Which platform is best?

It depends on how often you update content, who updates it and what you need to integrate. We'll recommend the right fit during discovery.
