Care & growth

Updates, monitoring, analytics and continuous improvement, long after launch day.

Why it matters

Websites drift. Content goes stale, small issues pile up and opportunities get missed. A site that's actively cared for stays fast and secure, and keeps converting more of the visitors you already have.

How we approach it

Depending on your plan, we handle content updates, new pages and campaigns, monitoring, backups and security, plus analytics reviews that turn data into clear next steps. Urgent items like closures, alerts or outages get priority.

We also take over sites built by others. We'll review yours, tell you honestly what shape it's in and propose a plan.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Content and page updates
  • New pages and campaign landing pages
  • Alerts and announcements
  • Security, backups and monitoring
  • Performance tuning
  • Analytics and monthly reporting
  • SEO upkeep
  • Priority support

Questions

Do you maintain sites you didn't build?

Yes. We start with a review and share what we find before agreeing on a plan.

Care can be monthly or as needed. We'll recommend what fits how often your site changes.
