Signs it's time

  • You hesitate to send people to your own site.
  • It looks cramped or broken on phones.
  • Customers call to ask things the site should answer.
  • It's slow, or a headache to update.
  • Your services or brand have changed since it launched.

Rankings protected

The biggest risk in a redesign is losing search traffic. Before changing anything, I find the pages that already bring in visitors and what Google already knows about you. Those pages keep their value through preserved content, careful URL planning and proper redirects.

A clearer message

Most struggling sites have the same problem: visitors can't tell fast enough what you do, whether you serve them or how to reach you. I restructure around those questions, modernize the design and put the next step everywhere it belongs.

Lighter and easier to maintain

Years of plugins and patches slow a site down. A clean rebuild is faster, more secure and much easier to keep current.

Common questions

Straight answers before you get started.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

Not if it's done right. I map your existing pages, keep what's valuable and set up redirects so visitors and search engines land in the right place. A faster, clearer site often ranks better over time.

Can you keep my logo and branding?

Yes. I can work within your brand, refresh it lightly or help modernize it.

My site is on WordPress, Wix or Squarespace. Can you redesign it?

Yes. I'll recommend staying on that platform or moving to something faster and easier to maintain, based on what's best for you.

How long does a redesign take?

Anywhere from a few weeks to a few months, depending on size and how quickly content and feedback come together.
