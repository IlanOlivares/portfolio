Brand identity

Logos, visual systems and guidelines that make you recognizable everywhere you show up.

Why it matters

People judge credibility in seconds, and your brand is the first signal. A cohesive identity makes a small organization look established and a large one look intentional, across your website, social, signage and every email you send.

How we approach it

We start with positioning, then explore visual directions that express it. The chosen direction becomes a complete system: logo suite, color palette, typography, imagery guidance and components, documented so anyone can apply it consistently.

Because we also design and build websites, every identity we create is tested on screen from the beginning, not adapted as an afterthought.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Brand positioning workshop
  • Logo and wordmark suite
  • Color and typography system
  • Iconography and graphic elements
  • Imagery and tone guidance
  • Social and email templates
  • Brand guidelines
  • Web-ready asset library

Questions

Can you refresh our existing brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Many clients need an evolution, not a revolution. We can modernize what you have while keeping the recognition you've built.

Do we get the source files?

Yes. You receive the final logo files and brand assets in formats for web, print and social.
