A site that fits your business, not a template

Templates are built to fit everyone, so they fit no one especially well. A custom site starts with your business: who your customers are, what they need to know before they reach out, and what makes you the better choice. The design, the page structure and the words all follow from that.

The result looks established from the first screen, explains what you do in plain language, and makes it easy to call, book, buy, give or get directions.

Built by hand for speed and search

I code every site myself instead of stacking page-builder plugins. That means pages load quickly on phones, there's less to break, and search engines can read your content cleanly. Fast, well-structured sites tend to rank better and convert more of the visitors they get.

Every build includes the SEO foundations: unique page titles and descriptions, structured data that tells Google and AI assistants who you are and where you work, a sitemap, accessible markup and Google Search Console setup.

Who it's for

Custom design is a good fit if you're launching a new business, outgrowing a DIY site, or competing in a market where looking credible matters, like professional services, healthcare, real estate, churches and nonprofits. If you need your team to edit pages themselves, I can build on a platform like Webflow instead of hand-coded HTML.

What working together looks like

We start with a free consultation. You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and price. From there we plan the pages and content, I design and build, you review along the way, and I launch and stay on for support. You work with me directly the whole time.

Common questions

Straight answers about custom website design in El Paso.

How much does a custom website cost in El Paso?

Cost depends on the number of pages, who writes the content and any special features like booking, payments or online giving. After a free consultation you get a written proposal with a clear price before any work begins.

Do I need to have my content ready?

No. Most clients don't. I'll help you plan what each page needs to say and edit or shape the copy so it reads clearly and works for search.

Will I be able to update my site myself?

If editing it yourself matters, I can build on a platform like Webflow that's designed for that. Otherwise I handle updates for you, quickly, through a care arrangement or as needed.

Do you work with businesses outside El Paso?

Yes. I'm based in El Paso and also work with clients in Las Cruces, across Texas and New Mexico, and nationwide by video call.

Ready for a website that looks as good as your work?

Tell me about your business and what you need. I'll reply personally with next steps and an honest take on whether I'm the right fit.
