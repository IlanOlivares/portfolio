Strategy & UX

Research, positioning, site architecture and user journeys, so every page has a job to do.

Why it matters

Most underperforming websites have a strategy problem, not a design problem. Visitors can't tell quickly what you do, whether you're for them or what to do next. Strategy fixes that at the root, so design and development have a clear brief.

How we approach it

We start with your goals and your audience, review analytics and competitors, and map the questions customers ask before they buy. From there we build the information architecture, page-by-page content plan and conversion paths that the rest of the project follows.

Strategy is included in every website engagement and is also available on its own, as a roadmap your internal team can execute.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Stakeholder and customer discovery
  • Analytics and competitor review
  • Positioning and messaging
  • Information architecture and sitemap
  • User journeys and conversion paths
  • Content plan and page briefs
  • Wireframes
  • Measurement plan

Questions

Do we need a strategy phase for a small site?

Yes, scaled to size. Even a five-page site benefits from clear messaging and a defined path to contact. For smaller projects it may be a single working session.

Can you deliver strategy only?

Yes. We can deliver a strategy and sitemap your team implements, or carry it through design and build.
