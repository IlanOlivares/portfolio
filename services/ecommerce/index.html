E-commerce

Online stores that sell, with checkout, payments, shipping and inventory handled properly.

Why it matters

In e-commerce, every extra second and every confusing step costs sales. Product presentation, search, mobile checkout and trust signals make the difference between a visit and an order.

How we approach it

We usually build on Shopify for its reliability and ecosystem, or custom when your needs call for it. We design product and collection pages that sell, configure payments, taxes and shipping, connect inventory and fulfillment tools, and set up analytics so you can see what's working.

What's included

Scoped to your needs. Every engagement starts with a written proposal.

  • Store strategy and catalog structure
  • Custom storefront design
  • Shopify setup and theme development
  • Product and collection templates
  • Payments, taxes and shipping
  • Inventory and fulfillment integrations
  • Product SEO and structured data
  • Conversion and analytics tracking

Questions

Do you work with Shopify?

Yes. Shopify is our default recommendation for most stores. We also build custom solutions when a project requires them.

Can you migrate our existing store?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and orders where possible, and set up redirects to protect your search rankings.
