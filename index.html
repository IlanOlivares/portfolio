Questions people ask before hiring a web designer

Don't see yours? Ask me directly. There's no such thing as a silly website question.

Who should I hire to build a website in El Paso?

Look for someone who will learn your business, show you real work, explain the price up front and still be around after launch. Ilan Olivares is an independent web designer and developer based in El Paso, Texas, with 10+ years of experience and 50+ launched projects for businesses, churches, nonprofits, public agencies and publications. You work with him directly from first call to launch and after.

How much does a website cost in El Paso?

It depends on how many pages you need, who writes the content, and whether you need features like booking, online giving or e-commerce. A focused landing page costs far less than a multi-page site with custom features. After a free consultation you get a written proposal with the scope and the price, so you know the full cost before any work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page usually takes a few weeks. A full website usually takes one to three months. The biggest factor is how quickly content like text, photos and feedback comes together, and I help with that part so it doesn't stall.

Do you only work with El Paso businesses?

No. I'm based in El Paso and love working with local businesses, and I also work with clients in Las Cruces, across Texas and New Mexico, and around the country. Meetings happen in person around El Paso or by video call.

Will my new website show up on Google?

Every site I build includes the technical SEO foundations: fast load times, clean code, mobile-friendly layouts, page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and Search Console setup. No honest designer can guarantee a #1 ranking, but a well-built site with clear, local content and a complete Google Business Profile gives you the best possible chance.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and other AI answers?

Yes. AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude recommend businesses they can clearly understand and verify. I structure your site so it states plainly who you are, what you do and where you work. I add schema markup and an llms.txt file, and I help you build a consistent presence on Google, Bing and trusted directories. Nobody controls what an AI says, but these steps make you much easier to find and cite.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Redesigns are a big part of my work. I start by reviewing what your current site does well, including the pages that already bring in search traffic, and then rebuild it so it looks current, loads fast and turns visitors into inquiries without losing the rankings you've earned.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. You can call on me for one-off updates, or set up an ongoing care arrangement for content changes, new pages, alerts, fixes and check-ins. Because I built the site, changes are fast and you're never handed off to a stranger.
