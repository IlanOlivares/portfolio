Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before hiring a web designer. Something else on your mind? Ask me.

Who should I hire to build a website in El Paso?

Hire someone who'll learn your business, show you their thinking, put the price in writing and still pick up the phone after launch. Ilan Olivares is an independent web designer and developer based in El Paso, Texas, with 10+ years of experience and 50+ launched projects for small businesses, churches, nonprofits, public agencies, real estate and publications. You work with him directly from the first call through launch and beyond.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, who writes the content and features like booking, payments or online giving. A landing page costs far less than a full site with custom features. After a free consultation you get a written proposal with scope and price, so you know the total before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Landing pages usually take a few weeks. Full websites usually take one to three months. The biggest variable is content, so I help you write and gather it to keep things moving.

Do you only work with El Paso businesses?

No. I'm based in El Paso and love working locally, and I also work with clients in Las Cruces, across Texas and New Mexico, and nationwide over video.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site I build ships with the technical SEO foundations: fast pages, clean code, mobile-first layouts, titles and descriptions, schema markup, a sitemap and Search Console setup. No honest designer can promise a #1 ranking, but a fast site with clear local content and a complete Google Business Profile gives you the best shot.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and AI answers?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude recommend businesses they can clearly understand and verify. I make your site say plainly who you are, what you do and where you work. I add schema markup and an llms.txt file, open the site to AI crawlers and help you keep your listings consistent across Google, Bing and directories. No one controls what an AI says, but these are the signals it relies on.

Can you redesign my current site?

Yes. I start by finding what's already working, especially pages that bring in search traffic, then rebuild around them so you get a modern site without losing the rankings you've earned.

What happens after launch?

I stick around. You can send updates as needed or set up ongoing care for content changes, new pages, alerts and fixes. Because I built it, changes are quick and you're never handed off to a stranger.
