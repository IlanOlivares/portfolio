How much does a website cost in El Paso?

A plain-English guide to what drives the price of a website, and how to compare quotes without getting burned.

If you've asked around, you've probably heard wildly different numbers for a website, from a few hundred dollars to tens of thousands. Both can be "right," because "a website" can mean very different things. Here's what actually drives the cost, so you can compare quotes fairly and spend on what matters for your business.

What sets the price

How many pages, and how different they are

A one-page landing page is the smallest project. A small business site usually has a home page, a page per main service, an about page and a contact page. Every unique page layout adds design and build time. Pages that share a template, like individual service pages, cost less each.

Who writes the content

Content is the most underestimated part of any website. If you provide finished text and photos, the project moves faster. If you need help writing, shaping or translating copy, or need photos sourced, that's real work and belongs in the quote.

Custom design or a template

Templates are cheaper up front but look like everyone else and are often slower. Custom design costs more because it's built around your customers and brand, and it usually performs better in search and conversions.

Features

Online booking, payments, e-commerce, donation forms, member areas, event calendars and integrations with other software all add time. Simple contact forms and click-to-call are standard.

Languages

In El Paso, many businesses need English and Spanish. A bilingual site needs translated content and the right technical setup, so search engines show each visitor the correct language.

SEO and ongoing care

Technical SEO foundations should be included in any professional build. Ongoing local SEO, content updates and maintenance are usually a separate monthly or as-needed cost.

Costs beyond the build

  • Domain name: a small yearly fee.
  • Hosting: anywhere from free for simple static sites to a monthly fee for platforms like Webflow, WordPress hosting or Shopify.
  • Email: business email through Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 is billed per user.
  • Updates and care: as needed, or a monthly arrangement.

How to compare quotes

  • Make sure each quote covers the same pages, content help and features.
  • Ask who owns the site, domain and content when it's done.
  • Ask what happens after launch, and what updates cost.
  • Ask whether SEO basics, mobile design and speed are included or extra.
  • Ask who will actually do the work.

Website cost questions

How much does a website cost in El Paso?

It depends mainly on the number of pages, who writes the content, custom design versus a template, special features like booking or payments, languages and ongoing support. A landing page costs far less than a multi-page custom site. A written quote based on your specific needs is the only reliable number.

Is a cheap website worth it?

Sometimes, for a very simple need. But a slow, generic or hard-to-update site can cost more in lost customers than it saves. Compare what's included, not just the price.

Do I have to pay monthly for a website?

Usually there are small ongoing costs for the domain and hosting, and optional costs for updates and maintenance. The build itself is typically a one-time project cost.
